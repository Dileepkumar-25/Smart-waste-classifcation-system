      The Global Recycling Crisis

      Over 2 billion tons of municipal solid waste is generated globally each year, with at least 33% not managed in an environmentally safe manner. Contamination is the single biggest bottleneck: when non-recyclables or greasy food packaging contaminate recycling streams, entire batches are rejected and diverted directly to landfills.

      EcoSort AI addresses this issue directly at the household and organizational source. By leveraging edge computer vision models running client-side, EcoSort AI delivers instant, reliable classification and actionable preparation steps to ensure clean, high-grade recyclables.
